Terms of use

The short version of how Planet Pilot works between you and us, in plain words.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Who makes the game

Planet Pilot is made and published by Postify Games. The game itself is proprietary and its copyright belongs to Postify Games. You are free to play it as the game and these terms allow.

Buying and refunds

You buy Planet Pilot, and its free demo, through Steam. The Steam Subscriber Agreement governs the purchase, including Steam's own rules on refunds.

Fair play online

Single player is yours to enjoy however you like. In online play (Dogfights PvP and co-op) please:

  • do not cheat: no hacks, no modified game files or network traffic to gain an advantage;
  • do not attack, overload or probe the game server, and do not send automated or repeated problem reports;
  • play the match through: do not wreck other people's games on purpose.

Callsigns are assigned from two fixed word lists, and there is no chat, so there are no names or messages to moderate. If you break these rules we may stop you from using the online features.

Online features

Online play and problem reports run on a server we operate. It may be unavailable at times, for example during an update or an outage, and online features may change or end one day. Single player does not need them and works offline.

Support

Questions about the game or your purchase: support@planetpilot.world. Bugs: bugs@planetpilot.world or "Report a problem" in the game's Settings. See the support page.

Your data

What the game and server collect, and how to have it deleted, is in the privacy policy.

Open data

The map databases derived from OpenStreetMap are offered under their own licences (the Open Database License for the databases, CC BY-SA for two 3D models), not under these terms. See the open data page.

Changes

We will post any change on this page with a new date.
