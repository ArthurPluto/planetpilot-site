The game

A whole planet you can fly around

Planet Pilot shrinks our Earth to one fifth of its size, keeps it real, and paints it in bright cartoon colours. Take off from a grass strip, cross a continent before dinner, and land wherever you like.

The whole world, pocket-sized

Planet Pilot shrinks Earth to one fifth of its size, so you can really fly around it, while every plane, runway and building stays full size. Real coastlines, mountains, rivers and roads, nearly 2,900 real airports with their real runway layouts, and 57 cities rebuilt from real map data: more than 1.6 million buildings, with parks and trees.

  • Famous skylines, and 138 landmarks and mountains, 42 of them in high detail
  • A real sun, stars and city lights
  • Wind and weather that change every landing

A flying career

Start with a little ultralight and a few coins. Fly passengers, cargo, VIP charters, medical flights, photo flights and skydivers, or take the aerobatic plane to airshows and air races. Work your way up through 17 civil aircraft, from bush planes and business jets to a double-deck superjumbo and a supersonic jet.

  • 8 kinds of jobs
  • Upgrade engines, fuel, cabin and short-field kits in your workshop
  • A hangar and a logbook of every flight

Real flying, friendly controls

Flaps, landing gear, speed brakes, crosswinds and a proper stall, all tuned to feel right on a keyboard or a gamepad. Interactive lessons teach you step by step.

  • Every plane has its own autoland: set a destination and the autopilot flies the approach and lands
  • Every touchdown gets a score
  • Mind the city: buildings and landmarks are solid, though you can still fly under the bridges

Dogfights

A second game in the box. Fly from a squadron base against the Corsair air pirates: clear the skies, defend outposts, strike targets, intercept bombers, fly escort, hunt convoys and SAM sites, bust bunkers, then take on their boss aces.

  • 15 fighters, from First World War biplanes to modern jets
  • Cannons, rockets, bombs, heatseekers, radar missiles and glide bombs, plus flares and chaff
  • Online: duel a friend 1v1, fight 2v2 team battles, or fly co-op missions with 2 to 4 pilots

Legends

Fly 14 famous air battles where they happened, in a plane of their era, from the First World War to the jet age. Each one has its own twist, a short history card, a medal and a paint scheme to earn.

Achievements and a secret

70 achievements across the career and Dogfights. Earn 50 of them to unlock a secret hypersonic plane that flies at the edge of space.

FAQ

Questions, answered

When does Planet Pilot come out?

Tuesday, December 1, 2026, on Steam. The Steam page opens in early November, so you can wishlist it and get a note on launch day.

Which platforms is it on?

Windows PC, through Steam. It plays with a keyboard and mouse or a gamepad.

How much does it cost?

$19.99 USD, with a 15% launch discount in the first week. Regional prices on Steam vary.

Is there a demo?

Yes. A free demo comes to the Steam page around November 10, 2026.

Which languages does it support?

English, Hebrew, Simplified Chinese, Russian, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese (interface and subtitles).

Can I play with friends?

Yes: 1v1 duels and 2v2 team battles in online PvP, and co-op missions for 2 to 4 pilots. Online rooms are all-access: every Dogfights plane and weapon for everyone. Single player works offline.

Do I need to know how to fly?

Not at all. Interactive lessons teach you step by step, and every plane has its own autoland. The real flight physics are there when you want to fly it properly.

What PC do I need?

Minimum: Windows 10 64-bit, a 4-core CPU (Core i5 7th generation or Ryzen 3 1300X), 8 GB RAM and a Vulkan 1.2 GPU with 4 GB (GTX 1050 Ti or RX 570 class), 4 GB of storage. Recommended: Core i5 10th generation or Ryzen 5 3600, 16 GB RAM, RTX 2060 or RX 6600, and 7 GB on an SSD with the optional HD terrain.
