IN SHORT
Fly a toy-sized Earth with nearly 2,900 real airports, 57 real cities and famous
skylines. Climb from a tiny ultralight to a supersonic jet in a relaxed flying
career, then swap passengers for guns in Dogfights: missions, 14 Legends battles,
online PvP and co-op.

KEY FACTS
- World: Earth at one fifth scale with full-size aircraft, runways and buildings
- Nearly 2,900 real airports with their real runway layouts
- 57 cities from real map data with more than 1.6 million buildings, parks and trees
- 138 landmarks and mountains, 42 of them in high detail
- 17 civil aircraft (15 career planes, an aerobatic plane and a secret hypersonic
  plane) and 15 Dogfights fighters from First World War biplanes to modern jets
- Career with 8 kinds of jobs, workshop upgrades and a hangar
- Dogfights missions and boss aces, and 14 Legends battles
- Online PvP (1v1 and 2v2) and co-op for 2 to 4 pilots
- 70 achievements; 50 of them unlock the secret plane
- Interactive lessons, an autoland for every plane, solid buildings and landmarks
