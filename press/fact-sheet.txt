IN SHORT
A premium-cartoon flight game on a toy-sized Earth. Fly a planet one fifth of the
real size with about 2,890 real airports, grow a flying career from an ultralight
to a supersonic jet, then trade passengers for guns in Dogfights, Legends battles
and online PvP.

KEY FACTS
- A toy-sized Earth at 1/5 scale with real coastlines, mountains, rivers and cities
- About 2,890 real airports
- 268,000 real buildings across 8 detailed cities, plus 138 landmarks
- 17 career planes from an ultralight to a supersonic jet
- Dogfights: 15 fighters from WW1 biplanes to modern jets
- Legends: 14 famous air battles
- Online PvP and co-op
- 70 achievements and a secret plane
- Real flight physics, with per-plane autoland for new pilots
