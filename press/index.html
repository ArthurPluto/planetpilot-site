Fact sheet as plain text

Short description

Fly a toy-sized Earth with about 2,890 real airports. Grow from a tiny ultralight to a supersonic jet in a relaxed flight career, then trade passengers for guns in Dogfights, 14 legendary air battles, and online PvP and co-op.

Key facts

  • A toy-sized Earth at 1/5 scale with real coastlines, mountains and rivers
  • About 2,890 real airports
  • 268,000 real buildings in 8 detailed cities, and 138 landmarks
  • 17 career planes, from ultralight to supersonic
  • Dogfights with 15 fighters, and 14 Legends battles
  • Online PvP and co-op
  • 70 achievements and a secret plane
  • Real flight physics, with per-plane autoland

Long description

Planet Pilot shrinks our planet to a size you can actually fly around. The whole Earth is there at one fifth of its real size: real coastlines, mountains, rivers and cities, about 2,890 real airports, 268,000 real buildings in 8 detailed cities and 138 landmarks, all painted in a bright, premium cartoon style. A day and night cycle with a real sun, stars and city lights, plus wind and weather, make every flight a little different.

The career starts with a tiny ultralight and a few coins. Players fly passengers, cargo, VIPs, medical runs, photo flights and skydivers, and earn their way up through 17 career planes, from bush planes and business jets to a double-deck superjumbo and a supersonic jet, upgrading each one in the hangar.

Under the friendly look is a real flight model: flaps, gear, speed brakes, crosswinds and a proper stall, tuned to feel right on a keyboard or a gamepad. Newcomers are never stuck, because every plane has its own autoland that flies the circuit and lands. Every touchdown is scored, so there is always a smoother landing to chase.

Dogfights is a second game in the box: take off from a squadron base and fight the Corsair air pirates in 15 fighters, from WW1 biplanes to modern jets, with cannons, rockets, bombs, missiles, flares and chaff. Legends puts players in 14 famous air battles, where they happened, in a plane of their era. Online PvP and co-op bring friends along, and 70 achievements and a secret plane wait for the most dedicated pilots.

Planet Pilot is made by Arthur Getz, a solo developer, and comes to Steam for Windows on December 1, 2026, for $19.99, with a free demo in November.

About the developer

Arthur Getz is an independent developer who builds Planet Pilot on his own, from the flight model and the toy planet to the art direction. Contact: press@planetpilot.world.
