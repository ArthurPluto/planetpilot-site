The game

A whole planet you can fly around

Planet Pilot shrinks our Earth to one fifth of its size, keeps it real, and paints it in bright cartoon colours. Take off from a grass strip, cross a continent before dinner, and land wherever you like.

The whole world, pocket-sized

Real coastlines, mountains, rivers and cities on a planet at 1/5 scale. About 2,890 real airports, from grass strips in the mountains to the busiest hubs on Earth, and 268,000 real buildings in 8 detailed cities.

  • 138 landmarks to find and fly past
  • A day and night cycle with a real sun, stars and city lights
  • Wind and weather that change how every landing feels

A flying career

Start with a little ultralight and a few coins. Fly passengers, cargo, VIPs, medical runs, photo flights and skydivers, and earn your way up through 17 career planes, from bush planes and business jets to a double-deck superjumbo and a supersonic jet.

  • Upgrade every plane in your hangar
  • A logbook of every flight and every touchdown
  • 70 achievements, and a secret plane to unlock

Real flying, friendly controls

Flaps, landing gear, speed brakes, crosswinds and a proper stall, all tuned to feel right on a keyboard or a gamepad. Real flight physics for those who want it, and help for those who do not.

  • Per-plane autoland: press a key and it flies the circuit and lands
  • Every touchdown gets a score, so there is always a smoother landing to chase
  • Keyboard, mouse and gamepad

Dogfights

A second game in the box. Take off from a squadron base and fight the Corsair air pirates: clear the skies, defend an outpost, intercept bombers, hunt convoys and bust bunkers.

  • 15 fighters, from WW1 biplanes to modern jets
  • Cannons, rockets, bombs, heatseekers, radar missiles, flares and chaff
  • Beat the aces to unlock their rides

Legends

Fly 14 famous air battles where they happened, in a plane of their era, from 1917 to 1982. Each one has its own twist, a short history card, a medal and a paint scheme to earn.

Play together

Duel a friend or fight in teams in online PvP, or take on missions side by side in co-op.

FAQ

Questions, answered

When does Planet Pilot come out?

Tuesday, December 1, 2026, on Steam. The Steam page opens in early November, so you can wishlist it and get a note on launch day.

Which platforms is it on?

Windows PC, through Steam. It plays with a keyboard and mouse or a gamepad.

How much does it cost?

$19.99 USD (regional prices on Steam vary).

Is there a demo?

Yes. A free demo comes to the Steam page around November 10, 2026.

Which languages does it support?

At launch: English, Hebrew, Simplified Chinese, Russian, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese (interface and subtitles).

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Online PvP lets you duel a friend or fight in teams, and co-op lets you take on Dogfights missions together.

Do I need to know how to fly?

Not at all. Every plane has its own autoland, and the controls are tuned to be friendly. The real flight physics are there when you want to fly it properly.

What PC do I need?

Minimum: Windows 10 64-bit, a 4-core CPU, 8 GB RAM and a Vulkan GPU with 2 GB (GTX 960 or RX 470 class). Recommended: 16 GB RAM and an RTX 2060 or RX 6600 class GPU. About 3 GB of storage.
