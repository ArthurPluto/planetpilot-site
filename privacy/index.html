Privacy policy

Planet Pilot collects very little. This page says exactly what, why, and how to have it deleted.

Draft, to be reviewed. This text has not yet been checked by a lawyer and may change before launch.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Planet Pilot (the game) and planetpilot.world (this website) are made by Arthur Getz, an independent developer based in Israel, who is the data controller for the processing described here. Contact for anything about privacy: privacy@planetpilot.world.

2. The short version

  • The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers.
  • The game has no account of its own, no ads and no analytics.
  • A problem report is sent only when you choose to send it.
  • Online play needs a connection to our game server, which sees your IP address and your Steam name while you play.
  • We keep what we receive for at most 90 days, we never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

3. This website

planetpilot.world is a static website. It sets no cookies, uses no local storage and loads no analytics or advertising scripts. Fonts and images are served from the site itself.

Like every web host, the company that serves the site (currently GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc.) receives technical data needed to deliver the pages, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and may keep it in server logs for security. We do not receive or use these logs.

The trailer is a YouTube video. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play; it then loads from youtube-nocookie.com, and Google's privacy policy applies to that video.

4. The game

4.1 Saves and settings

Your career, settings, logbook and game logs are stored on your own computer (and in Steam Cloud if you use it). We cannot see them.

4.2 Optional problem and crash reports

When the game crashes, or when you open "Report a problem", it offers to send a report. Nothing is sent unless you agree. A report contains:

  • the game version and build, and whether safe mode was on;
  • your operating system name and version, and your system locale and game language;
  • the renderer and graphics card details (GPU name, vendor, graphics API and driver version);
  • your screen size and window size;
  • a random session id created by the game (not linked to you, your Steam account or your computer);
  • your comment, if you write one (up to 4,000 characters; please do not put personal details in it);
  • the last 64 KB of the game's log file, with your home folder path masked so your Windows user name is not included.

The report goes over an encrypted connection to our game server. The server uses your IP address for an hour, in memory only, to limit how many reports one address can send, and does not save it with the report.

Why: to find and fix bugs and crashes. Legal basis: your consent, given when you choose to send (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it by asking us to delete a report.

4.3 Online PvP and co-op

To play online, the game connects to our game server. While you are connected, the server processes:

  • your Steam display name (shown to the other players in the match);
  • your IP address (needed to send and receive game data, and to protect the server from abuse);
  • the live game state of your match (positions, hits, scores).

Match data lives in memory and is dropped when the match ends. Technical server logs, which may contain your IP address and Steam name, are kept for security and troubleshooting for up to 90 days.

Legal basis: providing the online mode you asked for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in keeping the server safe (Article 6(1)(f)).

4.4 Steam

Planet Pilot is sold and launched through Steam. Purchases, achievements, Steam Cloud saves, friends and invites are handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy. We only receive the standard sales and usage statistics Valve gives every developer, which do not identify you.

5. How long we keep it

Problem reports and server logs are deleted after at most 90 days. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to answer you, and then deleted within 90 days unless the conversation is still going.

6. Who we share it with

We never sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. The only third parties involved are the service providers that host our server, this website and our email, acting on our instructions, and authorities when the law requires it. Some providers are outside your country (for example in the United States or the European Union); where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, such transfers rely on adequacy decisions or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • stop or limit using it, or object to its use.

Email privacy@planetpilot.world. To find your problem report, include the report id the game showed you, or the date and time you sent it and your comment. We answer within 30 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, these rights come from the GDPR and the UK GDPR, and you may complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). If you are in Israel, they come from the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, and you may contact the Privacy Protection Authority. We would be glad to hear from you first.

8. Children

Planet Pilot is a game for a general audience, but the online features and problem reports are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. In some countries the age for consent is higher (up to 16 in parts of the EU). If you believe a child has sent us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data in transit is encrypted. Stored reports are on a server that only the developer can reach, behind an access key. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we collect small so there is little to protect.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. A significant change will also be noted in the game's update notes.
