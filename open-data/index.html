Open data

Planet Pilot's cities, roads, parks and waters are built from OpenStreetMap. The databases we derived from it are yours to download and reuse under the same open licence.

The offer

The game ships several databases derived from OpenStreetMap data, which we obtained through the releases of the Overture Maps Foundation. Under section 4.6 of the Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0, we offer each of these Derivative Databases, in full and in the exact form the game reads them, under the ODbL v1.0. You may copy, share and adapt them, including commercially, as long as you credit the sources below, keep them under the ODbL (share-alike), and do not lock them behind technical restrictions.

The game itself (its art, code, aircraft and gameplay) is a Produced Work and is not covered by this offer.

The databases

  • BuildingsDownload
    Building footprints with heights for the detailed city regions, one file per region plus an index (buildings/<region>.bin, buildings/index.bin), and the tower table city_towers.json. Each footprint keeps its real shape, size, orientation and height; the bake selects the buildings that fit the 1/5-scale ground, tallest and most important first. Script: tools/fetch_heights.py, tools/bake_city_buildings.py. Source: Overture Maps buildings, release 2026-09-23.1.
  • Roads: the road graph and road profilesDownload
    The connected road network the game's traffic drives on: motorways and trunk roads worldwide, primary roads around large towns, and secondary roads and named avenues in the detailed cities (roadgraph.bin, with the city main roads in roads.bin). The road profiles (roadprof/) hold the deck heights, bridges, viaducts and tunnels of every graph edge, solved on the game's terrain. Scripts: tools/bake_roads.py, tools/bake_road_graph.py, tools/bake_road_profiles.gd (run by tools/bake_road_profiles_par.py). Source: Overture Maps transportation/segment, release 2026-09-23.1.
  • GreenDownload
    Parks, gardens, grass, woods, cemeteries, golf courses, park ponds and tree points in the detailed city regions, one file per region plus an index (green/<region>.bin, green/index.bin). Script: tools/bake_city_green.py. Source: Overture Maps base land_use and land, release 2026-09-23.1.
  • City waterDownload
    Real-width city rivers, canals, docks and lakes, generalised for the 1/5-scale planet as a signed distance field (city_water.bin), plus the lake shore patches near the detailed cities. Scripts: tools/bake_city_water.py, tools/patch_lake_osm.py. Source: Overture Maps base.water, release 2026-09-23.0.
  • CoastDownload
    Coast patches that turn tidal city water (harbours, estuaries, tidal rivers) into sea, so a city's river and its harbour are one body of water (coast_ov_osm.bin). Script: tools/bake_city_water.py. Source: Overture Maps base.water, release 2026-09-23.0.
  • SOURCES.txtDownload
    Every file's source snapshot, the command that rebuilds it and the date it was baked.

File formats and bake scripts

The files are compact little-endian binary formats built for the game (tiles of quantised points, zlib or zstd compressed). Each format is documented byte by byte at the top of the script that writes it, and the scripts read the Overture releases straight from the public Overture S3 bucket with DuckDB, so anyone can rebuild the databases from the same snapshots.
